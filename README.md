# Hi, I'm Isaac Mwendwa Muthui 👋

**Founder & CTO, [Tamalaki Business Network](https://tamalakibn.com)** · Nairobi, Kenya

I build AI-native platforms for African industry, across agriculture, manufacturing, construction, exports and capital. At Tamalaki Business Network, a Nairobi-based innovation holding company, I lead technology across our ventures and work to keep them connected through shared data, supply chains and systems.
